Research

I'm interested in commutative algebra and its ever growing intersection with algebraic topology. In particular, I'm interested in using DG, derived, simplicial, and spectral methods to understand the homological and homotopical properties of commutative rings, especially in the study of singularities of local commutative rings.

This includes derived categories, algebraic and topological André–Quillen (co)homology, cohomological support theory, complete intersection and Gorenstein singularities, the homotopy Lie algebra, rational homotopy theory, Poincaré duality, (operadic) Koszul duality, and homological perturbation theory.
